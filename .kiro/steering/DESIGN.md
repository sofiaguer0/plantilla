## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an airy, modern editorial sensibility tailored for contemporary womenswear. Designed for young adult women navigating personal and career milestones, the visual language balances effortless youthfulness with poised maturity. It departs decisively from the saturated, heavy camel/beige aesthetic ubiquitous in the DTC apparel space, leaning instead into clean, luminous breathing room, crisp structural typography, and delicate botanical pastel accents.

The aesthetic leans on high-fashion minimalism elevated by soft, tactile precision:
- **Atmospheric Clarity:** Broad expanses of crisp off-white canvas and unhurried whitespace allow garments and editorial photography to command focus.
- **Understated Poise:** Sophisticated serif display headers paired with clean, geometric grotesque body copy deliver an elevated magazine aesthetic that remains effortless to navigate on mobile devices.
- **Muted Radiance:** Accents of light dusty sage and soft lilac-blush are used sparingly as atmospheric tints, indicators, and micro-delights, ensuring the product feels fresh and feminine without descending into novelty or juvenile sweetness.

## Colors

The palette establishes an open, luminous space defined by crisp, natural off-whites and grounded by a refined, desaturated charcoal for high-legibility typographic contrast.

- **Primary (`#212427`):** Deep Studio Charcoal. Replaces harsh pure black with a softer, ink-like neutral that anchors high-fashion typography, primary CTAs, borders, and structured UI frames.
- **Secondary (`#6C8275`):** Dusty Sage. A calm, grounded botanical green used for supportive badges, category indicators, highlights, and secondary actions that evoke freshness and organic tailoring.
- **Tertiary (`#A78B95`):** Soft Wisteria Blush. A muted, sophisticated lilac-mauve utilized for promotional moments, subtle tags, editorial badges, and interactive hover states.
- **Neutral (`#F8F8F6`):** Crisp Chalk. A clean, softly illuminated off-white surface that eliminates eye fatigue while maintaining sharp edge definition against product imagery and editorial card containers.

### Extended Palette Guidelines
- **Canvas Base:** `#F8F8F6` (Chalk White)
- **Surface Elevation 1:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White, used for product image backdrops and modal card containers)
- **Surface Subdued:** `#EFF0EB` (Soft Pistachio Mist, used for filters, input fills, and inactive tabs)
- **Text Primary:** `#212427`
- **Text Secondary / Muted:** `#62666B`
- **Hairline / Border:** `#E2E4DE`

## Typography

The type system blends the high-contrast elegance of **Playfair Display** with the sharp, contemporary utility of **Hanken Grotesk**. 

- **Headlines & Editorial Titles:** Playfair Display introduces literary charm, luxury craft, and high-fashion rhythm. Headings are spaced with slight negative tracking to retain density and poise.
- **Body, UI, & Functional Layers:** Hanken Grotesk offers crisp readability across e-commerce product grids, detail specs, filters, and transaction flows. Its modern grotesque geometry grounds the romantic qualities of the serif headers.
- **Labels & Micro-Copy:** Uppercase treatments paired with expanded tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) are strictly reserved for categories, badges, currency indicators, and size selectors to provide unmistakable navigational signposting.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a responsive fluid grid anchored by expansive outer margins that simulate the white borders of luxury printed lookbooks.

### Grid Structure
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid. Max container width capped at 1440px for editorial continuity. Outer canvas margins set to `margin` (3rem), with `gutter` (1.5rem). Product catalog matrices typically leverage 3 or 4 columns; story spreads break into alternating 5/7 column asymmetric balances.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column fluid grid. Outer margins scale to 2rem; gutters maintain 1rem. Product grids transition to 2 or 3 columns.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4-column fluid grid. Margins compress to `margin-mobile` (1.25rem) with tight `gutter-mobile` (0.75rem) to preserve vertical flow while maintaining thumb tap safety margins. E-commerce listings utilize an alternating 2-column card stack with full-width hero breakouts.

### Spacing Principles
- Vertical rhythm favors generous white space between functional modules (`space-xl`), allowing looks and collections to breathe without feeling sparse or disconnected.
- Component internals (buttons, badges, inputs) remain tight and disciplined (`space-xs` to `space-md`), balancing spacious macro-layouts with dense, decisive micro-interactions.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through quiet tonal layering, delicate hair-thin outlines, and soft ambient light, avoiding heavy dropshadows or skeuomorphic bevels.

- **Tonal Layering:** The primary canvas sits at `#F8F8F6`. Raised surfaces such as cards, sheets, and slide-over drawers use pure `#FFFFFF`. Recessed zones, input fields, and pill selectors shift down to `#EFF0EB`.
- **Low-Contrast Hairlines:** Modals, product cards, and floating navbars are outlined with an ultra-fine, crisp border (`1px solid #E2E4DE`).
- **Diffused Ambient Shadows:** Floating elements (such as sticky quick-add bars, navigation headers on scroll, and drawer overlays) utilize an ultra-diffused, tinted shadow that prevents muddy greys:
  - *Floating Nav / Tooltips:* `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(33, 36, 39, 0.04);`
  - *Slide-out Drawers / Menus:* `box-shadow: -12px 0 40px -4px rgba(33, 36, 39, 0.07);`
- **Translucent Frost:** The sticky header and mobile navigation use subtle background frost (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px); background-color: rgba(248, 248, 246, 0.82);`), letting lookbook photography soften gracefully beneath interface controls.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Soft (`1`)** geometry to communicate tailoring precision, clean architectural seams, and elevated maturity.

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px):** Applied to form inputs, small tags, image thumbnails, and buttons. This provides subtle warmth at corners while keeping lines clean and architectural.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg`, 0.5rem / 8px):** Reserved for modal sheets, drawer tops, and product collection containers.
- **Extra Large Radius (`rounded-xl`, 0.75rem / 12px):** Used exclusively for floating popovers and floating banner modules.
- **Exception (Full Capsule / Pill):** Swatches, size selector pills, and category filter chips may leverage fully rounded pill profiles (`9999px`) to visually separate functional tactile filters from content containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Studio Charcoal (`#212427`) background with Crisp Chalk (`#F8F8F6`) typography in `label-lg` uppercase. Padding: `12px 28px`. Corners use 4px soft radius. Hover transition subtly shifts background to `#3A3F44`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background enclosed by a 1px border of Deep Studio Charcoal (`#212427`). Text in `#212427`. Hover fills with `#EFF0EB`.
- **Tertiary / Link:** Pure typographic action with an offset bottom hairline border (`1px solid currentColor`), shifting to Dusty Sage (`#6C8275`) on hover.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Height: 32px. Border-radius: pill (`9999px`) or soft 4px.
- Inactive state uses a clean `#FFFFFF` background with a `#E2E4DE` hairline border and `body-sm` text.
- Active state transitions to Dusty Sage tint (`#6C8275` background, `#FFFFFF` text) or Deep Charcoal depending on priority.

### Lists & Navigation Links
- Navigation links sit in `label-lg` or `body-md` with expansive click targets (minimum 44px height).
- Cart and checkout lists feature hairline bottom dividers (`#E2E4DE`), left-aligned product thumbnails with subtle 4px corner rounding, and right-aligned numeric pricing.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Precision 18px squares/circles with a 1px border (`#C8CCC4`).
- Checked state fills with Charcoal (`#212427`) or Dusty Sage (`#6C8275`) with an interior white checkmark or dot. Focus rings present a subtle 2px offset halo tinted in Pistachio (`rgba(108, 130, 117, 0.2)`).

### Input Fields
- Understated light background (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border in `#E2E4DE`.
- Height: 44px (desktop) / 48px (mobile).
- Typography: `body-md` in `#212427`, with placeholders in `#8F949B`.
- Focus state maintains the 1px stroke, transitioning to Deep Charcoal (`#212427`) without abrupt high-glow outer shadows.

### Cards (Product & Editorial)
- Minimalist framing: borderless product imagery set against pure chalk backgrounds with an aspect ratio of 3:4 or 4:5.
- Subtle metadata layout beneath the image: Product title in `body-md`, category in `label-sm` muted text, price in `title-sm`.
- Floating color swatches (12px circular discs) anchored with a 1px stroke border. Quick-add overlays slide in smoothly from the bottom edge using low-elevation ambient shadows.

### Specialized Apparel Components
- **Size Selector Grid:** Modular square or capsule tiles (36px x 36px). Disabled sizes display a delicate diagonal strike-through hairline (`#D1D5CB`).
- **Color Swatch Selector:** Concentric rings where an active swatch has an outer ring separated by a 2px white gap.
- **Editorial Lookbook Hotspots:** Minimalist 24px translucent discs with pulsing center rings overlaying editorial photography to showcase garment details on demand.
